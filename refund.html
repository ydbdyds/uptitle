退款说明

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

1. 14 天无理由退款

买断，以及年度会员的首次付款，扣款后 14 天内都可以申请全额退款，不需要说明理由。年度会员的续费不退款。

2. 怎么申请

发邮件到 ydbdyds@gmail.com，写上购买时用的邮箱或订单号。退款通过 Lemon Squeezy 退回原付款方式，通常 10 天内到账，具体时间看银行。

3. 退款以后

对应的许可证会停用，App 在下次联网核验时回到免费版。免费功能照常能用，你 Mac 上的录音、字幕、笔记和词本都保留。

4. 取消年度会员

在 Lemon Squeezy 的订单页 app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders 用购买邮箱登录即可取消。续费的款项不退，不想续费请在续费日前至少 48 小时取消。取消后不再扣款，会员一直用到已付费期间结束。

5. 超过 14 天

一般不再退款。如果遇到重复购买、取消后仍被扣款这类问题，随时联系我们，我们会处理。

6. 其他

  • 反复购买再退款的，我们可能拒绝退款。
  • 有问题请先联系我们，再考虑向银行申请拒付；我们通常处理得更快。
  • 本说明不影响你所在地法律给予消费者的权利。

Refund Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. 14-day refunds, no reason needed

For a one-time purchase, and for the first payment of a yearly membership, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the charge without giving a reason. Renewals of a yearly membership are not refundable.

2. How to ask

Email ydbdyds@gmail.com with the email address you used to buy or your order number. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy and usually arrive within 10 days, depending on your bank.

3. After a refund

The license is deactivated, and the app returns to the free version at its next online check. The free features keep working, and the recordings, transcripts, notes and vocabulary book on your Mac stay where they are.

4. Canceling a yearly membership

Sign in with your purchase email on Lemon Squeezy’s order page, app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders, to cancel. Renewal charges are not refunded, so if you don’t want to renew, cancel at least 48 hours before the renewal date. After you cancel there are no further charges, and membership continues until the end of the period you paid for.

5. After 14 days

Refunds are generally not available. If something went wrong, such as a duplicate purchase or a charge after you canceled, contact us at any time and we will sort it out.

6. Other

  • We may decline refunds for repeated purchase-and-refund cycles.
  • Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually resolve it faster.
  • This policy does not affect consumer rights you have under the law where you live.
